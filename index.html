Survey Observation

Based on the survey represented as 100 respondents, 33% of individuals know why waste segregation is important. Only 25% always segregate waste before disposal, while 58% never do so.

The most commonly generated household waste is food/kitchen waste at 58%. Waste collection is reported as daily by 58% of respondents, with lack of awareness reported by 33% and poor waste segregation by 17%. 50% are willing to improve their waste segregation practices.

Overall Findings & Suggestions

Core insights gathered from field analytics alongside key policy and community suggestions.

♻️ Waste Segregation
Waste should be separated at source into appropriate categories such as wet, dry and recyclable waste.
🏠 Community Participation
Community participation plays an important role in improving waste management.
🌱 Environmental Protection
Better segregation and recycling can help reduce environmental pollution.
📢 Public Awareness
Regular awareness programs can encourage responsible waste disposal practices.
📝

Recommendations

  • Promote segregation of waste at source.
  • Provide separate bins for different waste categories.
  • Increase awareness through community campaigns.
  • Encourage recycling and reuse.
  • Improve collection and processing facilities.
  • Promote responsible disposal of hazardous waste.
