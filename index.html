Survey Observation

Based on the survey of 100 respondents, there is strong baseline environmental awareness with 72% of individuals conscious of segregation principles. While 61% currently segregate waste on a regular basis, there remains an actionable gap of 39% who practice it occasionally or not at all.

The majority of solid waste comprises organic food waste (45%) followed by recyclable plastics (25%). Furthermore, an overwhelming 84% expressed active willingness to support and cooperate with improved waste management infrastructure and door-to-door collection initiatives.

Recommendations

  • Promote segregation of waste at source.
  • Provide separate bins for different waste categories.
  • Increase awareness through community campaigns.
  • Encourage recycling and reuse.
  • Improve collection and processing facilities.
  • Promote responsible disposal of hazardous waste.
